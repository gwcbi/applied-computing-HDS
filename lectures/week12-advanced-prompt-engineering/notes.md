## Lecture outline

1. **Integrating AI tools into the development environment** (25 min) —
   in-IDE assistants (Copilot-style), agentic coding tools, how they differ
   from chat-based use covered in Week 11. Live demo of AI-assisted
   development inside VS Code/RStudio.
2. **Hallucination detection and validation** (30 min) — concrete
   strategies: asking for citations/sources and checking them, testing
   generated code against known cases, cross-checking numeric claims.
   Live demo: a real example of an AI tool confidently generating an
   incorrect statistic or a nonexistent package/function, and how a
   careful user would catch it.
3. **Building a personal validation habit** (10 min) — connect to the
   course's AI policy (declared use + documented oversight); this is the
   lecture that makes "oversight" concrete rather than a policy line item.

## Practical session (Nov 11)

- Project work time with a structured task: each team identifies one place
  in their project where they used AI output without full verification,
  and verifies it now, documenting the process.
