I can't give you a number I'd trust here. I can't query the cohort, and I don't remember the exact figure. Stage IV is a small subset of the TCGA PanCancer Atlas LUAD cohort (about 25 patients), so the median is unstable and depends on how stage is grouped (IV vs. IVA/IVB) and on censoring.

My rough recollection is about 15 months, but treat that as a guess, not a result.

To get the exact value, open the LUAD PanCancer Atlas study in cBioPortal. Then go to Clinical Attributes, group by Neoplasm Disease Stage American Joint Committee on Cancer Code, and read the Kaplan-Meier median OS for stage IV.
