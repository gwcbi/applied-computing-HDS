## Lecture outline

1. **Reframing Weeks 1–10 through an AI lens** (10 min) — students have
   already been using AI with declared/documented assistance since Lab 1;
   this module makes that practice explicit and systematic rather than
   introducing something new.
2. **Prompt engineering fundamentals** (25 min) — clear task specification,
   providing context/examples (few-shot), specifying output format,
   iterative refinement. Ground every principle in a health-data example.
3. **Task decomposition and prompt iteration** (20 min) — breaking a large
   ambiguous ask ("clean this dataset") into smaller, verifiable prompts;
   live demo iterating on a prompt that initially gives a wrong/incomplete
   answer.
4. **Tool landscape** (20 min) — Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini for
   analysis tasks: not a feature comparison, but when each modality fits
   (in-IDE completion vs. chat-based reasoning vs. agentic multi-step
   tasks).

## Practical session (Nov 4)

- Hands-on prompt-iteration exercise: give students a deliberately
  underspecified task, have them prompt an AI tool, evaluate the output,
  and revise the prompt at least twice — instrumenting the habit of
  iteration rather than accepting the first response.
