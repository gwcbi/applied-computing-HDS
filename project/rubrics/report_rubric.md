# Final Report Rubric (35% of final grade)

Report format is typically a journal manuscript (Intro/Methods/Results/
Discussion or equivalent) for PUBH 6854; other formats need prior
instructor approval. PUBH 4201 students prepare a poster by default (see
`GRADING_AND_POLICIES.md`) and may opt into this report track instead.
Team reports must include a **Contributions** section (required for full
credit, not just recommended).

There's no fixed page-length target — length should scale with the
project's scope and complexity. Write enough to clearly document the
problem, methods, results, and AI use; padding to hit a page count isn't
rewarded, and a shorter report that covers everything clearly beats a
longer one that doesn't.

| Criterion | Weight | What "excellent" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Problem framing & motivation | 15% | Clear statement of the real-world question; motivates why it matters, grounded in relevant background/literature |
| Methods — computational | 25% | Methods are correct, appropriately chosen, and described in enough detail to reproduce |
| Methods — AI use documentation | 15% | States which model(s)/agents used, key prompts, what was AI- vs. human-generated, and how AI output was validated or corrected |
| Results | 20% | Results directly answer the stated problem; figures/tables are clear and correctly interpreted |
| Reproducibility | 15% | A third party could re-run the analysis from the repo (environment file, instructions, data access documented) |
| Writing & organization | 10% | Clear, well-organized, appropriate for the chosen format; Contributions section present and specific (teams) |

**Scoring scale per criterion:** Exceeds (100%) / Meets (85%) / Partially
meets (65%) / Does not meet (<50%) of that criterion's weight.
